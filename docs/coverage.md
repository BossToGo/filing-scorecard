# Coverage report (slice S3)

Generated by `app/scripts/coverage.py` on 2026-11-18 from SEC data retrieved through the app's SEC client (cached in `app/.cache/sec/`). Do not edit by hand: change the rules, then run the script again.

## Method

- **Sample:** the first 100 companies of the SEC ticker list, in the SEC's own order (largest first), that are not a second share class, not a bank, insurer or real estate trust (SEC industry codes 6000 to 6799) and that file Form 10-K reports. No hand-picking. Skipped while selecting: 64 (listed at the end).
- **Industries:** 27 SEC major groups (first two digits of the industry code) in the sample; SPEC asks for at least 8.
- **Resolved** means the resolver (`app/src/data/resolver.py`) gives a reported or derived figure, never a guess. The go/no-go uses the latest fiscal year (FY0); the five-year column is stricter.
- **Limitation:** the sample is made of very large companies; smaller companies may file less complete data. The rules are the same for any ticker; companies outside the verified universe are shown as "not verified" (SPEC §2).

### Option for the group: operating income where the income statement has no such line

16 companies file no operating income for their latest fiscal year: their income statements go straight to pre-tax income (e.g. ADP, BMY, COP, CVX, DE, ETN). If the group allows operating income = pre-tax income + interest expense for them (labelled `derived`, an approximation of EBIT that includes other non-operating items), 14 of them would resolve: operating income FY0 83% → 97%, and the SPEC §13.4 rule would give **go as planned**. Not built: it changes what P5, P6, H6 and V4 measure, so it is the group's decision (SPEC §6, `AGENTS.md` rule 1). Either way, the demo uses only the verified universe below.
