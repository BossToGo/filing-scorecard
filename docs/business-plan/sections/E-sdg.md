## Sustainable Development Goal 9

Seen through SDG 9, Filing Scorecard is a small piece of financial infrastructure: a bank can add it next to its order screen. It aims to make the bank's investment service more accessible, by giving every customer who invests the same analysis for free, and more transparent, because each figure comes from public data and can be checked at its source. The closest target is 9.5, which asks for innovation and stronger technological capabilities; our link to it is indirect, since 9.5 is mainly about research in industrial sectors. Target 8.10 (under Goal 8), on access to financial services for all, is related (United Nations General Assembly, 2017).

These public sources are the audited annual reports that US companies file with the SEC, interest rates from FRED and the industry averages that Professor Damodaran publishes. Since many investors already go through their bank (see Users, market and alternatives), the bank, a domestic financial institution, is well placed to offer this analysis.

However, our contribution to SDG 9 is modest: the tool is a prototype, not research, its impact has not been measured, and the demo covers only five of the thousands of US companies that file a 10-K. As for target 8.10, the tool only helps customers who already have a bank account and can invest, so it does not widen access itself. The pilot described in How the bank changes could measure whether it also reaches less experienced investors, for example through the share of scorecard users who had placed no order in the previous 12 months.
