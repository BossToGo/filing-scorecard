## Sustainable Development Goal

Our chosen target is 8.10, which aims to strengthen the capacity of domestic financial institutions to expand access to banking, insurance and financial services for all (United Nations General Assembly, 2017). Our client, a European retail bank, is such an institution: the tool gives it a new service that it can offer to every customer who invests.

The product is free for the customer (see Business model) and gives the same information to everyone, whether they are beginners or experienced (see How the bank changes). It is built on public data: the audited annual reports that US companies file with the SEC, interest rates from FRED and industry averages from Damodaran. Each figure is linked to its source, so it can be checked. Since many investors already go through their bank (67% of the investors surveyed by the AMF place their orders through a traditional bank; AMF, 2024), the bank is well placed to offer this information.

However, our contribution to this target is modest. The target is mainly about people who have no access to financial services, while our tool only reaches customers who already have a bank account and can already invest: it does not change the target's indicators, such as the share of adults with an account. It improves the information around a service that already exists, does not give advice and does not replace advisers. Its impact has not been measured: it is still a prototype, and the demo covers only five of the thousands of US companies that file a 10-K. The pilot described in How the bank changes could still check whether the tool reaches less experienced investors, for example by measuring the share of scorecard users who had placed no order in the previous 12 months.
